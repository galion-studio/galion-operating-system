One field

A labyrinth that ends at a dot.

Memory, voice, vision, emotion, and pictures are one path. Galion calls that path the maze of the brain, and then refuses to put the maze on the screen. The product surface of the voice is a single disc. It rests, it listens, and it speaks. Size and brightness are the whole interface.

Behind the dot, Obsidian Open Mind is the desktop layer: memory, tools, and agents on a CPU. Inside that desktop live two consumer products, Galion Research and Galion One. Both are free. Both are open source.

Galion OS is the operating system underneath. It is free, open source, and Linux-based. The image is in development. This repository is the document for it. A download will be listed when an image exists.

Status

What is true today.

The Galion OS repository holds documents: the direction, the two image profiles, and the one-dot surface spec. It does not hold a bootable image.

The voice repository holds an engineering program. A learned student model is not shipped. The learned emotion detector is in development. Until a listener is ratified, these pages do not say that a call was understood.

Open Mind is a Rust workspace for a CPU box, with its own public status. Read that repository for what is built and what is still a scaffold.

Plus and Ultra are planned. paid_tiers stays false.

Planned pricing

Local is free forever. Hosting is a plan.

Galion OS, Obsidian Open Mind, Galion Research, and Galion One are free and open source. On hardware you control, Galion Voice is a free program too. Plus and Ultra are optional hosted computers for a person without that hardware. The prices are the owner's planned prices. Billing is not implemented. paid_tiers stays false.
