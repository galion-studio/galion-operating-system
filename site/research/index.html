Galion Research

A research voice that shows its work.

Galion Research is the other consumer product inside the Obsidian desktop. It is curious, analytical, and free. It separates what was found from what was guessed, and it says which is which.

How it speaks

The answer, then the limit, then stop.

Galion Research is the voice. Hermes is the path behind it: knowledge, tools, and memory, off the 80 ms heartbeat of the voice. A lookup that is late is said to be late. A missing fact stays missing.

The earlier public name of this line is Hermes Research. The product introduces itself as Galion Research.

Interest can lift the pace when a fact is solid. Uncertainty slows it down. Both are delivery. A compliment is not a finding. “I don't know” is a result, and it comes first when it is true.

Where it sits

Inside the desktop. Free.

Galion Research is open source, the same as Galion One. It is not a film test, and it is not the companion. The companion is Galion One. The command register, Jarvis, stays a command register. A research question does not borrow either costume.

Empathy, when a grant allows it, is the same live process as the rest of the voice: pace, warmth, and a pause. The finding stays the finding. The learned emotion detector is in development.

A hosted research seat, when hosting exists, is the planned Plus or Ultra computer. On your own machine, Research is part of what you run for free. No hosted console is on sale from this page.

Read the Open Mind repository for what the CPU stack already builds, and read it with its own status table. Scaffolds are labelled there. This page does not promote a scaffold into a product you can switch on tonight.
