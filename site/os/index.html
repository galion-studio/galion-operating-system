Galion Operating System

An operating system that stays on the CPU.

Galion OS is free and open source. It is Linux-based. It is built for the processor, from a laptop down to a small board. MIT licensed. Your data stays on hardware you control.

Read the repository Planned pricing

The base

Linux, then a calm machine.

The system is small on purpose. Private by default means the machine in front of you is the one that holds the work. A GPU is not the premise. The CPU owns the loop.

Obsidian Open Mind is the desktop layer inside Galion OS. Memory, tools, and agents sit there. The owner calls this layer the Obsidian desktop. It is a Galion project, and it is a different project from the Obsidian note-taking app.

Inside that desktop live two consumer products. Galion Research looks things up and says what the looking found. Galion One is the companion. Both are free and open source.

Roadmap

Two profiles. The voice image is first.

Both profiles are in development. They share an init and the Open Mind contract. Neither image is published from this repository.

Galion Voice, the program, also runs on plain Linux. You do not need either image to work on the voice. The program's own page is Galion Voice.

This repository

Documents first.

What is in git today is the README, the licence, and the voice-profile notes: the first image, the later window, the one-dot framebuffer spec, and the packaging contract. There is no kernel build, no release archive, and no download button that would be honest.

When an image exists, the root README is where the download will be listed. Until then, the status is in development.

Planned pricing

Local is free forever. Hosting is a plan.

Galion OS, Obsidian Open Mind, Galion Research, and Galion One are free and open source. On hardware you control, Galion Voice is a free program too. Plus and Ultra are optional hosted computers for a person without that hardware. The prices are the owner's planned prices. Billing is not implemented. paid_tiers stays false.
